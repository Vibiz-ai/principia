# Launch readiness review

1. Read the launch scope and supporting project documents. Record what was actually checked and what remains unknown.
2. Review product readiness, installation or onboarding, documentation, support ownership, analytics, rollback and communications as relevant to this launch.
3. For each gap, state evidence, impact, owner if known and a concrete next action. Separate blockers from improvements.
4. Produce a go/no-go recommendation with explicit conditions; do not claim tests or approvals that have not happened.
5. Keep launch announcements as drafts and do not publish, deploy or change production settings unless instructed.
