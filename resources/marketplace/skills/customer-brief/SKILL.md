# Customer context brief

1. Identify the customer goal, recent interactions, unresolved requests, commitments and next meeting context from the provided sources.
2. Cite the source and date for each material claim. Separate verified facts from assumptions and stale information.
3. List open commitments with owner and due date only when stated. Call out missing context.
4. Prepare a one-page brief and three useful questions for the next conversation.
5. Do not invent account metrics, retrieve unrelated private records or send messages without an explicit request.
