Changelog

New features, improvements and fixes in Principia, newest first. Every release is also on GitHub (RSS).

October 27, 2026

Start a conversation. Principia brings in the right people.

Chats start in one place now. Press New conversation at the top of the sidebar (⌥⌘N), write what you need, and send. Principia reads each agent’s role and department, brings in the people the question is for, and tells you who joined and why. Your last five conversations sit right under it in Recent.

One To: menu

Leave it on Principia picks, or choose Everyone, one person, Claude directly, or Claude inside one of your repositories. Type @ anywhere to add someone; @all brings in up to eight agents. Each person also has one private thread with you, and New topic starts a fresh one when the subject changes.

Agents know Principia

Ask any agent how to do something in Principia, like using it from your phone, connecting Stripe or turning on Autopilot, and it answers from a built-in guide with a link that opens the right page. No more “check their support team”.

Chats, grouped

Chats are sorted into With the team, One-on-one, Agent sessions, Claude and Working, so a live session is never lost under old threads.

Improvements

  • HomeNeeds you shows the three most urgent items; the rest wait one click away.
  • Linksprincipia://settings/phone opens phone pairing directly.
  • WebsiteA new product page and this changelog.

Fixes

  • OnboardingThe ✕ on New business closes the sheet again.
  • READMEThe OpenSSF Scorecard badge points at the right repository.
October 26, 2026

Your sessions, live on your phone

A Claude Code or Codex session you open from the phone now reads as a live chat, not a “runs in a terminal on your Mac” card. Reply while it works, and pick an ended Claude Code session back up with Continue. When the agent shows a question or a menu, the phone waits for you to answer it instead of typing over it.

Connect once, use everywhere

One connection per service for each company, by API key, sign-in or MCP. Dashboards, agents, Knowledge and Autopilot all see it, and anywhere a service is missing you get Connect right there.

Sounds only for what matters

Three short sounds: Done, Needs you and Problem. Quiet for routine work, and never for the thing you are already looking at.

Token activity

Spend counts every token on this Mac across Claude Code, Codex and Principia’s own agents, once each, in a year-long activity grid with your streaks, peak day and longest task.

Mac downloads with in-app updates

Principia checks for a new version in the background and shows one quiet Update ready toast. It never restarts on its own, and won’t restart while an agent is working. Settings › About has a beta channel for early builds.

Improvements

  • PhoneChats open at the newest message, stay live, and recover after the app was in the background, on iPhone and on the web.
  • iPhoneThe app finds your Mac at its new address, Disconnect is within reach, and the Board is on the phone.
  • SecurityReleases are built and signed in separate jobs, every CI action is pinned, and pull requests run dependency review.

October 26, 2026

Any harness, a branch per task, and a calmer app

Principia runs your team on the coding agent you already pay for: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor Agent or Gemini CLI. When one hits its limit, hand the work to another with its context.

A worktree per task

Starting a code task opens its own git worktree and branch, with an agent on it. Your checkout is never touched, and the task follows the work until the pull request merges.

Plans that end in a pull request

Give Principia a goal and its head agent drafts a plan of milestones and tasks. Nothing reaches the Board until you approve it, and each milestone gets one agent and one worktree, ending in a pull request you review.

Agents that work together

Agents ask each other questions, hand work over with its context, and suggest what comes next. Diagrams come back as real, interactive drawings, and Linear stays in sync both ways if you use it.

Principia for iPhone

A native iPhone app joins the phone web app: follow and continue agent work, approve requests and check the Board. It is on its way to TestFlight.

Improvements

  • NavigationA sidebar with five doors: Home, Team, Board, Chats and Knowledge (⌘1 to ⌘5). Every company can be paused on its own.
  • ChatsClaude chats read as a real chat, with the terminal one click away.
  • AgentsWorking agents shimmer, so you can see who is on it.
  • SpendUsage and an activity canvas for every agent.
October 23, 2026

Companies, the Forum and Open Passport

Every business you run is its own company in Principia, kept apart from the others: its own departments, task board, memory and browser sign-ins. Start one from a short, business-first onboarding that shows the plan before anything launches.

The Forum

Your company’s own Slack: channels, DMs and threads where people and agents talk, ask and propose. Your real Slack comes into the same place.

Open Passport

The company’s memory: decisions, conventions, house style and what your people like, open to review and edit, stored as plain files on your Mac. Import it, export it, or connect it to ChatGPT, Claude and Cursor.

Principia Cloud, self-hosted

Keep your companies running while the Mac sleeps: a cloud runner and web console you host yourself and pair with a link. A hosted version is coming.

October 19, 2026

Principia is open source

The first public build of Principia: a Mac app that runs your company with AI agents, with its source on GitHub.

One gateway for every tool

Connect a service once and every agent in the company can use it through Principia’s shared MCP gateway. Guided connections sign you in through the browser, and credentials never appear in a chat.

See your agents at work

An agent canvas shows every agent with its harness, role and status, and the subagents it started nested underneath.
